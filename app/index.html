Japan in Numbers — an original statistics site. Figures are rounded published totals from the Statistics Bureau of Japan, the Cabinet Office, the Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare, the Ministry of Finance, the Bank of Japan, the World Bank, the IMF and the OECD; each figure names its source and year on its page.

Built for make.fun’s “Make the best national statistics site” bounty. The whole site is static files and works offline. Sources & methods
